# Anagrams

The letters of a French word are mixed up: put them back in order before time runs out. In pixel art.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## All the words and daily game (new in 1.2.0)

The **Mode** menu offers two new ways to play. **All the words**: seven letters, and every common word of 4 letters or more you can spell with them to find (between 8 and 24 per grid). The boxes show each word's length; Tab reveals a letter; any other real word counts as a bonus. No timer. **Daily game**: the same seven letters for everyone, new every day, with the streak of days finished in a row. Words come from Lexique 3.83 (lexique.org, CC BY-SA 4.0 licence).

## Controls

- **Keyboard**: type the letters, **Backspace** removes the last one.
- **Mouse**: click the letters, or a slot to remove.
- **Space**: shuffle again · **Tab**: a hint · **Escape**: pause.
- **Gamepad**: left and right pick a letter, **A** places it, **X** removes, **Y** gives a hint, **RB** shuffles.

## How it works

- 5 words per level, from **4 to 9 letters** as you go.
- Nearly **1700 common French words**, and each word you find shows **its definition** (in French).
- **Another word** using all the letters earns a **bonus**.
- Find words fast and without hints to build a **combo**: ×2, then ×3.
- A **hint** places the next letter but costs points; time left earns points.
- At the end of a level, a **recap**: each word, its time, the hints used, and 1 to 3 **stars**.
- **Fullscreen** button at the top right of the game.

## Data and sync

Your level, stars and records are saved by NeoDex and synced between your PCs if sync is on.
