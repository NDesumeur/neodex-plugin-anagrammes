# Anagrams

The letters of a French word are mixed up: put them back in order before time runs out. In pixel art.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Controls

- **Keyboard**: type the letters, **Backspace** removes the last one.
- **Mouse**: click the letters, or a slot to remove.
- **Space**: shuffle again · **Tab**: a hint · **Escape**: pause.
- **Gamepad**: left and right pick a letter, **A** places it, **X** removes, **Y** gives a hint, **RB** shuffles.

## How it works

- 5 words per level, from **4 to 9 letters** as you go.
- **Another word** using all the letters counts too.
- A **hint** places the next letter but costs points; time left earns points.

## Data and sync

Your level and records are saved by NeoDex and synced between your PCs if sync is on.
